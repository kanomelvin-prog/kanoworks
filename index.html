KanoWorks

AI products, designed and built for the built world.

Most product designers have never opened Civil 3D. Most people who live in it have never shipped a product. KanoWorks sits in the middle: a one-person studio that designs and builds AI products end to end, with a transportation designer's understanding of how infrastructure actually gets made.

Current work

  • Alo A therapeutic AI companion built around a safety-first architecture, now heading into a clinical pilot. alowen.com
  • Civil design tooling Verification and design tools for transportation engineering geometry, built by someone who has drawn it by hand for years. In development; details when it ships.
  • Case studies Written up as each product reaches a real first user. Nothing here is invented or projected; if a number isn't measured yet, it isn't on this page.

Why

The thread through all of it: tools that give everyday people, especially the ones the system tends to leave behind, the knowledge and leverage to move up. That's the filter every KanoWorks product has to pass.
